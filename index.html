President's Message

Several decades of dedicated service to the islands' business community

ANCCI represents large, medium and small businesses, trading houses and professionals. We take our members' concerns to local and central government, contribute economic studies and policy inputs, and build trade links with Thailand and South-East Asia under India's Act East policy.
