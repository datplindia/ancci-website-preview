The Andaman Nicobar Chamber of Commerce & Industry (ANCCI), established in 1962, is the premier organisation dedicated to safeguarding the vital interests of the business community across the Andaman & Nicobar Islands.

For over six decades, the Chamber has been the principal advocate for local enterprises, fostering friendly relations, mutual help and common understanding. ANCCI is the unified voice of its members, engaging proactively with the local administration and the central government. It represents the business community on all critical matters affecting the region's economy, including inland and foreign trade, commerce, tourism, shipping, transport, industry, banking and air services.

Registered as a Society in 1986 and renamed the Andaman Nicobar Chamber of Commerce & Industry in 2023, the Chamber continues its commitment to the sustainable economic growth of the islands.

Membership

As of December 2026, the Chamber has 909 registered members, with a core group of 460 active members across various trades. Firms, builders, contractors and associations are welcome to join.

Our affiliations

  • Confederation of Indian Industry (CII), Kolkata
  • Federation of Indian Chambers of Commerce & Industry (FICCI), New Delhi
  • Bharat Chamber of Commerce (BCC), Kolkata
  • The Southern India Chamber of Commerce and Industry (SICCI), Chennai
  • Indian Council of Arbitration (ICA), New Delhi
  • Confederation of All India Traders (CAIT), New Delhi
